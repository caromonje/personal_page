Philosophy

"Design is not decoration — it is the careful orchestration of meaning, material, and moment."

I believe in the quiet power of considered design. Every project begins with listening — understanding not just what needs to be communicated, but how it should feel. My work draws from the intersection of graphic design, event production, and strategic communication, allowing me to craft experiences that are visually precise and emotionally resonant.

Influenced by the restraint of Japanese aesthetics and the warmth of mid-century craft, I seek to create work that breathes. Negative space is not emptiness — it is an invitation. Typography is not text — it is voice. Color is not decoration — it is atmosphere.

Whether shaping a brand identity, coordinating a cultural event, or developing a communication strategy, my approach remains the same: strip away the unnecessary, honor the material, and let the work speak with clarity.
